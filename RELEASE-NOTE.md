## SUBJECT: "Updated Public Page"

On behalf of Ride Knox, I am pleased to announce that our updated public page has just been released. Our page now shows the updated ridership and station pressure story as of data collected from January - July 2026. The page still highlights the analysis from 2025 that the board saw, but now also displays the new headline: the Day Pass brought the Casual riders back that we lost from the price increase in 2025. For the 2026 story, we provide two prominent charts to illustrate how ridership and station pressure have changed.  Additionally, our page is readable by non-coders and available in a public repository on GitHub for anyone to view. Our public page also includes relevant links to the full memo from 2026, the full report from 2025, this release note, and our repository README.md file for those interested in the coding aspects.

Something to keep in mind when viewing our analyses is that the comparisons to 2025 were made with less data (trips) because 2026 has not ended. However, to the best of our ability, we have compared the 2026 to the appropriate 2025 baseline (in months). We recommend viewing the link to the 2026 Memo first, to see some of the key changes that have occurred and the improvements that the Day Pass have allowed for this year so far!
